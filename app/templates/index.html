                  Take your library with you

                  Export your library

                  Download everything you've logged here, whenever you like. The files include private notes and reviews, so keep them somewhere private.

                  Full backup

                  Every shelf, rating, review, journal entry, collection and custom tab, plus where you stopped. This is the file OmniTrackr can restore.

                  Spreadsheets

                  A ZIP of CSV files for Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets: your library, journal, collections, progress, and one file per custom tab.

                  Letterboxd

                  Your films in the layout Letterboxd's importer reads. Watched films keep their diary dates and rewatches. Ratings become half stars.

                  Goodreads or The StoryGraph

                  Your books in the Goodreads export layout, which both sites import. Finished books go on "read" and the rest on "to-read".

                  MyAnimeList and AniList match imports by their own ID numbers, which OmniTrackr doesn't keep, so there's no anime file for them yet. Your anime and manga are in the spreadsheets.

                  Bring your history with you

                  Import Studio

                  Bring your history from Letterboxd, IMDb, Goodreads, The StoryGraph, MyAnimeList, AniList, Kitsu, Trakt, or any spreadsheet. Upload the export file as it comes, ZIP included, and preview it before anything is saved. Existing records are never overwritten: matches and repeated rows are skipped.

                  Letterboxd ZIP IMDb CSV Goodreads CSV StoryGraph CSV MyAnimeList / Kitsu XML Trakt ZIP or JSON

                  Reads your public anime and manga lists straight from AniList, then shows the same preview. Nothing is saved until you confirm.

                  Map unusual spreadsheet columns

                  Optional. Match OmniTrackr fields to your file's headers. Choose at least Title when your spreadsheet uses a different name.

                  Choose a file to inspect its columns.

                  Migration guide

                  Confirm your import

                  Safety promise: preview performs no writes, imports are additive and atomic, imported reviews stay private, and artwork lookups are deferred until you visit the relevant library tab.

                  Tab Visibility

                  Customize which tabs you want to see in the main interface. Hidden tabs won't appear in your navigation, but your data will still be saved.

                  Account Deactivation

                  Deactivating your account will hide it from the system. You can reactivate within 90 days. After that, self-service reactivation is unavailable; contact support if you need deletion assistance.
