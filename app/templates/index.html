                                  Passkeys

                                  None yet

                                  Log in with your fingerprint, face or device PIN instead of typing a password. A passkey is made by your phone, computer or password manager for this site only, so it can't be guessed or phished, and it skips the two-factor code. Your password and Google keep working too.

                                    You haven't added a passkey yet. Add one on each device you log in from, or one that syncs through iCloud Keychain, Google Password Manager or 1Password.

                                    This browser can't make passkeys. Current versions of Safari, Chrome, Edge and Firefox can, on a phone or computer with a screen lock.

                                    That's the most passkeys an account can hold. Remove one you no longer use to add another.

                                    Take your library with you

                                    Export your library

                                    Download everything you've logged here, whenever you like. The files include private notes and reviews, so keep them somewhere private.

                                    Full backup

                                    Every shelf, rating, review, journal entry, collection and custom tab, plus where you stopped. This is the file OmniTrackr can restore.

                                    Spreadsheets

                                    A ZIP of CSV files for Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets: your library, journal, collections, progress, and one file per custom tab.

                                    Letterboxd

                                    Your films in the layout Letterboxd's importer reads. Watched films keep their diary dates and rewatches. Ratings become half stars.

                                    Goodreads or The StoryGraph

                                    Your books in the Goodreads export layout, which both sites import. Finished books go on "read" and the rest on "to-read".

                                    MyAnimeList and AniList match imports by their own ID numbers, which OmniTrackr doesn't keep, so there's no anime file for them yet. Your anime and manga are in the spreadsheets.

                                    Bring your history with you

                                    Import Studio

                                    Bring your history from Letterboxd, IMDb, Goodreads, The StoryGraph, MyAnimeList, AniList, Kitsu, Trakt, Spotify, Rate Your Music, Libib, your podcast app, or any spreadsheet. Upload the export file as it comes, ZIP included, and preview it before anything is saved. Existing records are never overwritten: matches and repeated rows are skipped.
