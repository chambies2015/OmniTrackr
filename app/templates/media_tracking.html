Why a Hub Helps

Media tracking is not one problem. A person tracking movies may care about ratings and directors, while a TV viewer cares about seasons, and a gamer cares about backlog status. The hub keeps those workflows connected so visitors can find the guide that matches their habits without guessing where to start.

It also gives public reviewers and search engines a clearer map of OmniTrackr's content. Instead of disconnected pages, the site now has a focused information architecture around personal media organization.
