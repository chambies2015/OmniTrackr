Keep OmniTrackr free

Support OmniTrackr

OmniTrackr is built and run by one developer, with no investors and no paywall. Every tracking feature is free and stays free. Supporters on Ko-fi pay for hosting and buy time for new features, and get a few thank-you extras.

What supporters get

♥ Supporter

A Supporter badge

On your public profile, with the month you started supporting.

♥ Supporter

The badge on every review

The same badge beside your name on every public review, on title pages, in the review directory and on your review pages, in your accent color.

A profile accent color

Eight colors for your profile page and badge. Gold unlocks for founding supporters and after six months of support.

A profile banner

Six banners for the top of your profile: aurora, film strip, pixels, pages, vinyl and stars. Each one takes your accent color.

Six showcase spots

Every member can pin three titles to the top of their profile. Supporters can pin six.

★ Founding supporter

Founding supporter, for good

Everyone whose support starts before 2027 keeps a Founding supporter badge and the gold color for as long as they support.

♥ Supporter · 6 months

Your months, counted

Your badge shows how many months you've supported, so a long-time supporter reads as one.

♥ your name

A spot on the wall

If you want it, your username goes on the supporter wall below, linked to your profile. It's off until you switch it on.

Every supporter also gets no ads if OmniTrackr ever shows ads to signed-in members.

Perks are cosmetic. Nothing about your library, privacy settings or data changes, and you can hide the badge any time.

How it works

  • Monthly membership on Ko-fi: perks stay on while the membership is active. Every tier gets the same perks, so pick what fits your budget.
  • One-time support: each one turns perks on for 30 days.

Perks switch on automatically when you use the same email on Ko-fi as on OmniTrackr. If your emails differ, copy your supporter code from Account → Supporter into your Ko-fi message. Then pick your color and choose whether to join the wall in the same place.
