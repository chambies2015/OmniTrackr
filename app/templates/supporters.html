Support OmniTrackr

OmniTrackr is free, and every tracking feature stays free. Supporters on Ko-fi help pay for hosting and new features.

What supporters get

  • A Supporter badge on your public profile, with the month you started supporting.
  • A profile accent color of your choice for your public profile page.
  • No ads if OmniTrackr ever shows ads to signed-in members.

Perks are cosmetic. Nothing about your library, privacy settings or data changes, and you can hide the badge any time.

How it works

  • Monthly membership on Ko-fi: perks stay on while the membership is active.
  • One-time support: each one turns perks on for 30 days.

Perks switch on automatically when you use the same email on Ko-fi as on OmniTrackr. If your emails differ, copy your supporter code from Account → Supporter into your Ko-fi message.
