Support OmniTrackr

OmniTrackr is built and run by one developer, with no investors and no paywall. Every tracking feature is free and stays free. Supporters on Ko-fi pay for hosting and buy time for new features, and get a few thank-you extras.

Where the money goes

There are no investors. The bills below are paid every month by the one person who runs OmniTrackr, and Ko-fi support is what covers them. Anything past the bills buys time for new features.

How it works

  • Monthly membership on Ko-fi: perks stay on while the membership is active, plus your tier's extras.
  • One-time support: each one turns the supporter perks on for 30 days.

Perks switch on automatically when you use the same email on Ko-fi as on OmniTrackr. If your emails differ, copy your supporter code from Account → Supporter into your Ko-fi message. Then pick your color and choose whether to join the wall in the same place.
