How it works

  • Monthly membership on Ko-fi: perks stay on while the membership is active, plus your tier's extras.
  • One-time support: each one turns the supporter perks on for 30 days.

Perks switch on automatically when you use the same email on Ko-fi as on OmniTrackr. If your emails differ, copy your supporter code from Account → Supporter into your Ko-fi message. Then pick your color and choose whether to join the wall in the same place.

Questions

Will anything I use now end up behind a paywall?

No. Every tracking feature is free for everyone and stays that way. Supporter perks are cosmetic: themes, colors, banners, frames, a badge and a couple of extra showcase spots.

Do I need an account to support?

No. Anyone can send a coffee on Ko-fi. If you have an OmniTrackr account, use the same email on Ko-fi and the perks switch on by themselves. If you sign up later with that email, they switch on then.

Who handles the payment?

Ko-fi does, with PayPal or a card. OmniTrackr never sees your card details. Ko-fi tells us the amount, the tier, the name you gave and the email you used, so the perks can find your account.

What happens if I cancel a membership?

Your perks stay on until the month you paid for ends. Your picks are saved, so if you come back they're all where you left them. Your library isn't touched either way.

I'd rather not see support notes in the app.

Members who haven't supported see a short note now and then, at most once every two months and only after something worth marking, like a reached goal. Press "Don't ask again" on the note, or switch it off in Account → Supporter.
