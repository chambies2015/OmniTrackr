What This Demo Shows

The demo is meant to answer a simple question: what does OmniTrackr actually do after signup? It shows the app's core value without requiring visitors or AdSense reviewers to create an account. Real accounts can keep categories private, choose which reviews are public, export data, and decide what friends can see.
