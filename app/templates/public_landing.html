Your home screen

Open the app and you see what you finished this month, what you’re partway through and how the year is going.

  • Counts for the month and the year
  • Covers of what you added last
  • A nudge when something you track has a new episode

A shelf for every format

Movies, TV, anime, games, books, music, manga, podcasts and audiobooks each get a shelf with covers, your score and a tick when you’ve finished.

  • Switch to a list to sort and filter
  • Search your own library
  • Add your own tabs for board games or anything else

Pick up where you stopped

Save the episode, chapter or page you got to. In this library Frieren is at season 1, episode 18, and one tap marks the next one.

  • Episode and page checkpoints
  • Rate single episodes and keep notes
  • Today’s pick from what you haven’t started

A diary of finishes and rewatches

Every finish lands on a calendar with its cover, and a count when it’s your second or third time through.

  • Log a rewatch or reread in one tap
  • Notes stay private
  • Show the diary on your profile only if you want to

Numbers about your taste

See how you rate, which decades you lean on and how far through each format you are. Time spent and Tasteprint are a tab away.

  • Rating spread and highest rated
  • Finished and still to go, by format
  • Hours spent across everything

Your year as a story

Year in Review turns the year into slides and a share image: what you finished, your busiest month, your top rated. It stays private unless you post it.

  • Plays like a story on your phone
  • Share card for the group chat
  • Private categories stay out of it
