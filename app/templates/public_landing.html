A useful habit, not another feed

From recommendation to a memory you can revisit

Start with the media you already care about. Add more as life happens, then let your own history become more useful over time.

  1. 01

    Save it

    Add a movie, show, anime, game, album, book, or your own custom category before the recommendation gets lost.

  2. 02

    Make it yours

    Track progress, use a rating scale that makes sense to you, and leave a quick note about why it worked—or didn’t.

  3. 03

    Log the moment

    Use the private journal when you start, make progress, finish, or revisit something—without changing the original library record.

  4. 04

    Find the pattern

    Review your weekly playback and Tasteprint to rediscover favorites, notice evolving habits, and share only the aggregate insights you choose.

A timeline that is yours

Log starts, progress, finishes, revisits, and private notes across every media type, then look back through one chronological journal.

Meet your Tasteprint

Turn completion, ratings, eras, creators, and journal rhythm into a private media portrait—with an optional share card rendered on your device.

Ask for one thoughtful pick

Send an expiring Recommendation Postcard to friends or share a guest link. Replies wait in a private inbox until you choose to save, add, queue, or dismiss them.
