How it works

From recommendation to a memory you can revisit

Start with a few things you already love. Add more as you watch, play, and read, and the history fills in on its own.

  1. 01

    Save it

    Add a movie, show, anime, game, album, book, or your own custom category before the recommendation gets lost.

  2. 02

    Make it yours

    Track progress, rate it on a scale that makes sense to you, and leave a quick note about why it worked (or didn’t).

  3. 03

    Log the moment

    Use the private journal when you start, make progress, finish, or revisit something. Journal entries don’t change the library record.

  4. 04

    Find the pattern

    Weekly playback and Tasteprint help you rediscover favorites and notice how your taste changes. Share only the insights you choose.
